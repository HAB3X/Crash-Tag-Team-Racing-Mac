# The texts said as a computer's

The game's lines about the PlayStation 2 (its memory card (8MB) and MEMORY CARD slots, the DUALSHOCK 2 analog controller and
its ports, the console) are said as a computer's, the way the Wrath of Cortex and Crash Twinsanity Mac ports do it
(`src/pctext.cpp`). Each original is checked first, in full, and each new line is written into the old line's own space
(every new line is shorter), so another executable or other data is left as it is. The memory card is "save data", the
console "your computer", the DUALSHOCK 2 "the controller", and no slot or port has a number.

Kept as they are: the buttons' names where a gamepad's button is pressed (START, the X button): a gamepad has them.

## The controller's message (the executable)

controller's (disconnected from either port, and from one port: the screen puts the port's number at the `$`). They're
changed before the boot. The seventh language is English in the USA's wording.

## The menus' lines (the text bibles)

Every other line is in the text bibles, Scrooby's, in the archives' `.p3d` files (`frontend` in COMMON.RCF's bootup.p3d and
FRONTEND.RCF's frontend.p3d, `onfoot` and `ingame` in the lands' archives, and `loading`): each language a chunk of UTF-16
lines found by a hash of their names. They're changed as each language is read (the reader at 0x370508, overridden): a line
that is one of the originals below, in full, is replaced. The game reads only the language in use (English on this disc).

Only the PS2 versions' lines are changed (named `ps2_...` in the bible, and six unnamed lines that say PlayStation 2, its
console or its MEMORY CARD slot): the bible also has the GameCube's, Xbox's and PSP's lines, which this game never shows. The French, German and
Spanish lines are older ones (they still name The Simpsons Hit & Run, which the new lines don't); the Dutch and Italian
bibles hold English lines or "???", and their English lines are the English ones below. Lines in those languages that name
no card, slot or console (a corrupt save in a save slot, "file not found") are kept.
